Educator self-reflection

Place yourself in your learners’ shoes.

Please complete the survey by reflecting on your own online or blended teaching and imagining the experience from your learners’ perspective. As you read each statement, place yourself in your learners’ shoes and consider how you think they would respond about your course. There are no right or wrong answers. The purpose is to support reflection on your current practice and to identify areas you may wish to explore further. Your scores for each CoI presence and subcategory will be calculated automatically and can be used as a starting point for reflection and discussion.

How the survey relates to the Praxis Framework: the established Community of Inquiry questionnaire is learner-facing, and its original question wording is retained here without changes. The original 34-item survey was developed and validated by a collaborative CoI research team; its multi-institutional validation is reported by Arbaugh and colleagues (2008). In this context, it is reframed as an educator reflective tool: you are being asked to place yourself in your learners’ shoes and anticipate how they might respond. The scores are prompts for reflection rather than an evaluation or diagnostic assessment. After completing the survey, you can select any category to examine the related approaches in the Praxis Framework.
